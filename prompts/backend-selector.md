# Backend selector

Choose one backend as the workspace source of truth:

1. `feishu_base` — first live-tested option; use a configured API/MCP connector or `lark-cli`.
2. `notion` — requires an external Notion API/MCP connector.
3. `obsidian` — local Markdown/YAML projection in an Obsidian vault.

State each backend's limitations before setup. Do not promise cross-backend bidirectional sync.
